Customer app · blueprint v0.3

A boutique you carry in your pocket

The design base for the Varnika customer PWA. The flows borrow what people already know from Swiggy, Zomato and District. The look follows Hyderabad's new Telugu cafes: a calm ivory ground, one elegant serif and a clean sans. The brand colours come straight from the Varnika monogram.

Five pillars

Every screen is checked against these. When two conflict, the one higher on the list wins.

Calm first

One serif, one sans, one accent colour. Ornament appears once per screen at most: an arch frame, a vine, an illustration. When in doubt, leave it out.

From: Hyderabad's Telugu cafes, where heritage is a quiet detail.

Thumb-first

Primary actions sit in the bottom third of the screen. The header holds the logo, chat, WhatsApp and the bag, the same on every screen. Touch targets are 44px or larger.

From: Swiggy's bottom nav and sticky action bars.

The garment sells

Photos sit in arch frames (3:4 in grids, 4:5 on the product page). Text stays short: name, fabric, price.

From: District's poster cards.

One next step

One wine-coloured action per screen. Once the bag has items, a slim bag bar follows you until checkout.

From: the Zomato "View cart" bar.

Always know where it is

If an order is in progress, it is the first thing on Home. The tracker shows real stages and a ready-by date. Chat and WhatsApp to the boutique are always one tap away in the header.

From: Swiggy's live order tracking, applied to a stitching order.

Colour

Taken from the logo: wine from the monogram, gold-brown from the bird and branch. Sage (from the boutique references) carries live and positive states, and blush (from the pink brand cards) appears only as a soft fill. Wine is the only colour for actions. The ground is always #F1F0EA.

Build plan

The console stays where it is. The store is a second Next.js 14 app in the same repo and imports the console's Supabase types, so there is one catalog.

Varnika/
├─ app/, components/, lib/        ← console (unchanged)
├─ lib/supabase/database.types.ts ← shared row types
└─ apps/store/                    ← customer PWA
   ├─ app/(tabs)/page.tsx         Home
   ├─ app/(tabs)/explore/         Category grid + filters
   ├─ app/(tabs)/stitch/          Book custom stitching
   ├─ app/(tabs)/orders/          List + [id] tracker
   ├─ app/p/[sku]/                Product page
   ├─ app/bag/                    Bag → checkout
   ├─ components/ui/              components above
   ├─ lib/tokens.css              this page's :root
   └─ public/brand/               logo, monogram, illustrations

1 · Foundation

  • Scaffold apps/store
  • Tokens, fonts, brand assets
  • App shell: logo bar, nav, bag bar

2 · Catalog

  • Public read policy on products
  • Home, Explore and Product page from listProducts()
  • Photos from image_url in arch frames

3 · Bag & orders

  • Local bag, then checkout creating an orders row
  • Phone OTP sign-in linked to customers
  • Tracker from order status and shipment milestones

4 · Stitch & PWA

  • Stitch flow linking the Fillout form
  • Chat on conversations
  • Manifest, install prompt, offline shell
Decisions needed before building
  • Currency: the console formats prices in USD and seed prices are 45–320. The prototype shows illustrative ₹ amounts.
  • Payments: UPI/Razorpay at checkout, or pay on confirmation over WhatsApp (as on your "How to order" card) for v1?
  • MRP / discounts: products has no MRP column. Add one, or drop the strike-through prices.
  • Tracker stages: should the console's "active" be split into cutting, stitching and finishing so customers see progress?
